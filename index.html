CASE DOSSIER & ARTIST STATEMENT

PROJECT TITLE If Being a Pretty Girl Is a Crime, I Will Be Sentenced to Life Imprisonment (如果靚女係罪，我將終身監禁)
ISSUING AGENCY IDPY (I Don't Proud Of You)
CHARGE Failing to Fulfill Bloodline Expectations & Refusing One-Dimensional Labels
TECHNIQUE Multi-Angle Photography, Digital Compositing & Color Grading (6 Works)

1. From "Pride Month" to "IDPY" (I Don't Proud Of You)

While June is celebrated globally as Pride Month for the queer community, the reality behind closed doors in many Asian households is the exact opposite. The fictional agency IDPY ("I Don't Proud Of You") is named after that familiar, crushing phrase Asian parents often use to suppress the next generation. When a child's gender identity diverges from traditional norms—especially when they cannot fulfill the heavy familial duty of continuing the bloodline—parental pride turns into silent disappointment and rejection. Paired with my model's reflection that transgender people are routinely "presumed guilty" by the public, IDPY embodies both familial suppression and societal policing.

2. Beyond a Single Label: Multi-Angle Compositing & Humanity

In society, the label "transgender" is often weaponized as a heavy, suffocating tag—reducing a complex human being to a single aspect of their identity, viewed purely through a judgmental, scrutinizing lens. To challenge this, these six photographs were created by capturing the model from multiple distinct angles (Front, Back, Left, Right, Top, Bottom), followed by digital compositing and expressive color grading. By layering different perspectives and hues into each frame, the work insists that transgender individuals are not defined solely by their transness; they are full, multifaceted human beings with depth, emotion, and beauty that refuse to be flattened by a one-sided gaze.
